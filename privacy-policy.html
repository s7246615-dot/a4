Privacy Policy

Comprehensive disclosure regarding student data protection, academic record retention, and algorithmic transparency at MetricBrainCliff.

1. Institutional Scope & Commitment

MetricBrainCliff operates this institutional website to present our cognitive neuroscience curricula, psychometric diagnostic assessments, and academic research publications. We collect limited digital telemetry and student communication records strictly to fulfill program admissions, respond to academic inquiries, and optimize catalog navigation. We adhere to rigorous global data protection standards and safeguard your private records against unauthorized commercial dissemination across digital platforms.

2. Categories of Information Collected

When you explore our educational curricula, request cognitive diagnostic assessments, or register for our scholarly research bulletins, we collect technical identifiers including Internet Protocol addresses, browser agent specifications, and navigation timestamps. For direct enrollment registrations or evaluation fees, we collect your name, email address, telephone contact, and payment details through encrypted processing gateways. We never sell, trade, or distribute your private records to commercial advertising networks.

3. Lawful Basis for Data Processing

Our data processing operations rest upon legitimate academic performance, contractual fulfillment for enrolled educational cohorts, and explicit student consent for scholarly newsletters. We retain customer transaction records solely for the duration mandated by municipal accounting standards and statutory auditing guidelines. Anonymized browsing telemetry is reviewed periodically to eliminate website navigation friction and improve mobile responsiveness across handheld mobile browsing devices and personal computers.

4. Security Safeguards & Data Transmission

We implement robust cryptographic safeguards including Secure Sockets Layer protocols, network firewalls, and credential controls to shield your submitted records. Access to student academic files is restricted strictly to authorized faculty members managing admissions logistics and psychometric evaluation. While comprehensive security protocols are enforced across our networks, internet transmissions carry inherent limitations, and students are encouraged to maintain confidential credentials.

5. Individual Rights & Regulatory Compliance

Depending upon your jurisdictional residency, you hold recognized statutory rights concerning your personal records, including the right to inspect, correct, export, or request deletion of stored files. You may also object to automated data processing or revoke consent for informational academic bulletins at any time without penalty. To exercise these individual privacy rights, submit a formal written request directly to our academic governance desk at our primary headquarters.

6. Privacy Desk & Contact Information

Our academic administration is committed to resolving all student data inquiries with complete transparency and prompt diligence. Please address your formal privacy communications to Data Protection Officer, MetricBrainCliff Institute, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone our support desk directly at +1-888-777-5845. We update this privacy policy periodically to reflect evolving data governance standards and technological benchmarks across global higher education.
