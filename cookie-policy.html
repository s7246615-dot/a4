Cookie Policy

Transparent disclosure of web cookies, analytics identifiers, and browser preference management at MetricBrainCliff.

1. Definition & Purpose of Cookies

MetricBrainCliff utilizes web cookies, local storage tokens, and comparable tracking mechanisms to deliver essential website functionality and analyze aggregate visitor traffic patterns. Cookies are miniature text files placed upon your browsing hardware that allow our servers to recognize returning scholars, preserve academic portal preferences, and maintain seamless enrollment workflows. This policy clarifies our cookie deployment methods and details your administrative controls across our online learning catalog.

2. Essential System Cookies

Essential cookies are strictly necessary to enable core website navigation, enforce HTTPS encryption protocols, and support interactive cohort registration operations. Without these fundamental system cookies, primary web services such as academic module selections and secure tuition transactions cannot function reliably. Because these operational files are essential to deliver requested digital educational services, they do not require optional prior consent under global electronic privacy frameworks.

3. Analytical Telemetry & Performance Metrics

Analytical and performance cookies collect aggregate, anonymized metrics regarding how visitors discover and navigate our cognitive research treatises and educational syllabi. We utilize Google Analytics (identifier G-0LY0HY7L01) to evaluate page response times, monitor geographic traffic trends, and identify potential navigation bottlenecks. These insights empower our academic engineering team to improve portal presentations, enhance mobile interface speeds, and maintain stable web server availability.

4. User Privacy & Browser Controls

We respect your digital autonomy and provide clear mechanisms to regulate cookie storage preferences across your desktop and mobile browsing devices. You may configure your internet browser settings to decline all optional cookies, notify you when files are transmitted, or delete previously stored browser cookies. Please be advised that disabling specific functional cookies may limit your ability to utilize certain interactive learning features within our online portal.

5. Technical Inquiries & Contact Desk

For detailed inquiries concerning our cookie governance procedures or to adjust your recorded preferences, contact our technical privacy coordinator directly. Written correspondence should be addressed to MetricBrainCliff Institute, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or communicated directly via telephone at +1-888-777-5845. We review our digital tracking architecture regularly to ensure ongoing alignment with evolving international privacy statutes and consumer protection directives.
